# Poiem: motion and a reason to return

**Phase A — strategy for graded review. No implementation approved yet.**

Prepared 5 October 2026 against `0a106d84` on `poiem-ui-audit`. This is a source-code audit, not a claim that browser, accessibility, device-performance or retention checks have passed. Phase A changes only this document. The existing UI-audit artifacts are untouched.

Poiem is a food journal for adults: **“A little tracking. A lot of living.”** It is not medical advice; retain the existing estimate/uncertainty and safety explanations. Scope is the signed-in daily product: Today, the log sheet, Photo, Describe, Manual, Review, Edit, Saved, Insights/Journey, You, Coach and Momo. Public welcome motion is outside this work. Onboarding and sign-in retain their compositions; only shared preference handling and the first-meal handover need integration.

The binding references are [ENGAGEMENT_PLAN.md, especially §§2, 7, 9 and the resolved choices](../ENGAGEMENT_PLAN.md), [the web design contract](../web/app/DESIGN.md), its [enforcement tests](../web/app/src/lib/designSystem.test.ts), and [the safety-resource review](safety/support-resource-review.md). The historical proposals in the engagement plan are subordinate to its resolved decisions: no capture-latency collection, no gems or purchases, no loss aversion, and staged presentation without random reward magnitude. Manual entry stays blank unless restoring an actual draft.

## 1. Diagnosis and intended feel

**Poiem has useful character and deterministic progress, but feedback is uneven: ordinary actions feel quiet while exceptional logs interrupt the task, and the logging-completion mechanic is disconnected from Today.**

The direction is a warm, capable kitchen: a press feels tangible, a saved entry settles into its place, Momo acknowledges the person, and one small piece of accumulated progress explains what comes next. The app should feel good to finish using. A longer session is not a success criterion.

Keep the present type, palette, spacing, flat controls and one hero per route. Add no resting tilt, raised cards or extra acid panels. Joy comes from a few purposeful movements and Momo's existing drawing, not another visual identity reset.

- [feel.ts](../web/app/src/lib/feel.ts) synthesizes sound and centrally gates vibration. Existing pairs: `select` + light, `open` + light, `close` + none, `log-confirm` + confirm, `badge`/`level-up` + celebrate, and `poke` + medium. Use this layer; never bypass it with another AudioContext or direct vibration.
- [useCountUp](../web/app/src/hooks/useCountUp.ts) has an OS reduced-motion fast path, but updates React text on animation frames. Keep it only where the effect has a purpose; don't add more simultaneous counters or replay calorie numbers on a visit.

### Prerequisites before richer motion

1. **Make Momo blind to nutrition outcomes.** HomePage line 103 passes `over` into [todayGreeting](../web/app/src/lib/todayGreeting.ts), line 45, changing dialogue and expression. Remove that input from the character path; keep over-guide information solely in the factual budget. Add a regression that changes calories/macros/targets while holding interaction context fixed and gets the same Momo response.
2. **Apply saved feel preferences before any route can emit a cue.** `feel.ts` defaults both channels on; the only synchronization is HomePage lines 121–125. A direct log/settings visit can therefore bypass saved off settings. Synchronize at the hydrated product/provider boundary, handle account changes, and suppress cues before preferences are known.
3. **Replace interrupting reward delivery.** [LogCelebration](../web/app/src/components/LogCelebration.tsx) currently traps focus, reveals each award at 420ms, and lasts at least 2,300ms plus 1,400ms for a piece; reduced motion still waits 1,800ms plus the piece time. Its callback-dependent timer can restart on parent renders. [LevelUpOverlay](../web/app/src/components/LevelUpOverlay.tsx) can precede it with another four-second modal and [Confetti](../web/app/src/components/Confetti.tsx) unconditionally draws 90 canvas particles. Replace the signed-in automatic modal chain with nonmodal, coalesced feedback; no canvas payoff is needed here.
4. **Restore the Day ring as the logging-completion mechanic.** [DayRing](../web/app/src/components/DayRing.tsx) exists but has no rendered importer; only the overlay consumes its calculation. Place it as a compact flat section beside/below Momo's existing note, not a second hero or another acid card. Required arcs follow Light/Regular/Detailed commitment; optional arcs stay explicitly optional. Its outcome must never read nutrition values.
5. **Correct reminder policy before proposing a return nudge.** The current copy and eligibility still protect a streak. The replacement is specified in §5; the two-a-day cap is a ceiling, not a target.
6. **Use accepted-action receipts, not timer proximity.** Today associates XP awards within two seconds of a meal event. Propose one pure feedback selector over the before/after action state with the exact entry ID and award keys. Add local activation guards where Manual, Saved and sheet shortcuts currently create a fresh ID on each tap. Keep persistence, cloud acknowledgement and recovery independent of animation.

Source caveat: cumulative logged days currently means distinct dates in the journal, not an immutable record of every day ever logged. Deleting the last entry on a date can lower that total; deleting a photo/favorite can also change an unclaimed feature-first condition. **A break does not change it.** Already owned pieces stay owned. Keep these existing semantics and make the display truthful; an independent permanent history would be a separate product/data decision, not a hidden addition in a motion pass.

## 3. Magic moments: deterministic, brief and usable

### A shared delivery rule

An accepted action updates product state and starts persistence immediately. Feedback acknowledges that state; it must not claim cloud sync has completed. Motion, audio and Momo are optional subscribers and never sit in the save path. Preserve existing offline/conflict/recovery notices. If saving fails, do not play success or manufacture progress.

Coalesce simultaneous first-log, ring, outfit, badge and level signals into **one** confirmation. Priority is first-ever meal → wardrobe arrival → logged-day milestone → ring closing → first daily log → ordinary confirmation. A level/badge already awarded can be mentioned in existing Insights, without a second takeover. Every eligible reward is still granted; presentation priority never changes eligibility, amounts, ownership or odds. Logging is acknowledged immediately even if art cannot run. While tracking is paused, a permitted save gets only factual confirmation/Undo: no reward choreography, progress prompt or automatic Momo reaction.

**Repeat taper overrides presentation priority.** For any automatic payoff attached to a second log that local day, cap movement at 240ms; for a third or later log, cap it at 120ms or use a static result. Even a coincident ring close or outfit arrival obeys that cap: show all factual outcomes immediately and let a deliberate later Try it on request invoke the longer wardrobe sequence. Ordinary second logs use `feel('select')` and third/later logs `feel('tap')`, lighter than the first log's confirm pattern. A combined event still plays just one cue. This reduces presentation only, never the reward itself or Undo availability.

Make visual acknowledgement once per accepted action ID. Ring closure is an incomplete→complete transition caused by a real log/detail action on the active local day, not hydration, a setting change or an imported snapshot. No replay on route remount, refresh, Undo/re-save or sync retry. First-ever uses a durable acknowledgement/migration rule rather than “the journal is currently empty”; deletion must not restart the first-run show. New presentation metadata, if required, is minimal and must follow the data-change rule in §7.

Use fixed-size artwork/status space and one polite, atomic success announcement. Screen readers get the settled result once, not every XP beat, count-up frame or decorative joke. Decorative layers have `aria-hidden` and `pointer-events: none`; feedback never takes focus. Undo appears immediately and retains its existing ten-second, focus/hover-paused availability. Important text and wardrobe access remain available after the visual beat ends.

### Motion vocabulary for this pass

- **Press:** existing `tactilePress` geometry, `motionMs.press` = 120ms maximum; use the existing 90ms `--k-press` depression for system buttons rather than stacking two transforms.
- **Read/confirm:** `motionFade` / `motionCssEase`, 240ms; short variants may use `motionMs.press`. Final text is accessible immediately.
- **Character reveal:** `bubblePop` = scale .94→1, y 6→0, opacity 0→1 with `motionFade`; `plateReveal`/`snapSpring` reuse `motionSoftSpring` (stiffness 220, damping 24, mass .9) on artwork only. Omit its rotation for the daily composition. Spring settling target is ≤480ms; if profiling misses that bound, use the existing fade tween. No business rule waits for settling.
- **Route:** take `pageWipe`/`motionStep`'s direction but omit rotation and limit travel to 12px; use `motionFade` for the 240ms daily transition. Animate the arriving content, never the fixed nav or entire shell. Same-day reopening uses only a 120ms fade and never restages the whole screen.
- **Idle:** existing `motionIdle` for one visible Momo artwork only, y travel ≤2px. No dashboard/card/icon loops.

Only **transform and opacity** animate. Static color selection and final geometry may update immediately. Meters must stop transitioning `width`; use a full-width fill moved with `translateX` inside an `overflow: hidden` track (not `scaleX`: scaling would squash a striped fill in proportion to its value). Day-ring values update immediately without tweening SVG stroke, mask, path or dash geometry; an opacity/scale acknowledgement on the existing ring/check artwork supplies closure. No animated height, border width, blur, shadow, chart path or full-screen canvas.

## 5. A small, honest reason to return

### Progress, anticipation and competence

Use **one progress note**, integrated with the existing Day ring/Momo area on Today. It points to the next existing cumulative logged-day milestone **or** deterministic logged-day outfit; never add another ladder beside them. Example: two real logged days are already progress toward the Pencil at three. A break preserves that progress. Show the name and criterion upfront, with no uncertain availability or odds.

Keep competence feedback concrete: a portion was corrected, a meal is now saved for easier reuse, or the existing breakfast comparison summarizes the person's own routine. Do not score food quality, weight change, restriction or logging speed. Use the existing [HabitMilestones](../web/app/src/components/HabitMilestones.tsx), [wardrobeProgress](../packages/product/src/wardrobe.ts) and Insights comparison instead of new cards, badges or daily tasks. Future feature-first outfits are visible in the dressing room, but Today should not urge someone to eat, drink or log artificially to unlock them.

Momo's warmth belongs in the settled note and deliberate interaction. Reuse the shared [voice corpus and exhaustive safety tests](../packages/product/src/mascotVoice.test.ts) for new lines. Keep character dialogue free of nutrition/body judgement and numbers; factual numeric progress belongs to the adjacent product status, not his voice. Consolidate Today's four-line cycle with existing content memory. Variety in a gesture, approved line or user-requested outfit is acceptable; variety in reward eligibility or amount is not.

### Reminder audit and proposed replacement

Today evaluates [web notifications](../web/app/src/lib/notifications.ts) on render/dependency changes, not through a closed-app scheduler. [Settings](../web/app/src/pages/SettingsPage.tsx), lines 296–297 and 455–458, exposes only an **Allow** permission action; there is no app opt-out, selected time or useful permission status. The adapter is silent and locally capped at two deliveries a day. It is not reliable background push.

The existing bodies include “keep your … streak going”, “still alive … keep it”, and “Freeze used. Streak safe”. [Shared eligibility](../packages/domain/src/notifications.ts), lines 64–71, specifically selects a `save` reminder when a streak has no freeze. Its copy test blocks nutrition language but currently allows loss framing. Correct both the policy and the examples; renaming the message is insufficient if the trigger still exists to save a streak.

Proposed replacement:

- **Off by default; explicitly optional.** A reminder row says “A gentle journal reminder” with Off/On, selected local time, delivery limitations and permission status. Ask browser permission only after the user requests On. Denied or unsupported states have clear copy and no repeated prompt. Provide an in-app Off control even if browser permission stays granted.
- **One chosen routine nudge per local day**, suppressed if already logged or tracking paused, with the existing **two-per-day global maximum retained**. No streak/freezes/target input decides whether it fires. Retire the automatic streak-save and freeze-safety messages from this experience.
- Prefer an explicit chosen time to inferred eating patterns. The current caller passes every meal hour despite the adapter's “first-log over 14 days” documentation. Do not build more personalization on that mismatch.
- Suggested copy: “A little journal time, if it fits your evening.” Alternative: “Your journal is here whenever you're ready.” Settings explanation: “Optional. Never about calories. Turn it off whenever you like.” No counts, missing-day labels, urgency, guilt or promised rewards.
- This pass can make **foreground eligibility and controls** correct. Do not promise delivery while the browser is closed, queue catch-up notifications after an absence, or introduce a push/backend service here. Reliable background delivery is a separately specified capability. Warm copy remains available without notifications.

## 6. Calm, Standard and Lively

**Standard is the default for new users.** Put a single “App feel” choice in You → Everyday preferences, using existing radio/filter primitives and immediate, explicit save feedback. This controls the daily interface, not food goals or logging commitment. Keep Show Momo, Mute Momo, Reduce Momo motion, Sound and Haptics as distinct controls; choosing an intensity must never turn an independently disabled channel back on.

| Dimension | Calm | Standard | Lively |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary press / route movement | Static selected/focus state; instant values; optional ≤120ms opacity acknowledgement; no scale/travel or stagger. | Existing 90–120ms press; 240ms route/sheet fade with bounded travel. | Same control timings as Standard; no slower input or bigger displacement of text. |
| Log / closure feedback | Text + row + polite result; no automatic character motion. | One brief moment: first/day/ring ≤480ms; first-ever/outfit ≤720ms. | Same duration ceilings; one extra small artwork mark or existing arm gesture, not extra reward beats. |
| Repeat logging | Static acknowledgement + toast/Undo. | Every second-log payoff ≤240ms; third onward ≤120ms, including coincident closure/unlock. | Same taper and immediate saves; repeat use does not become louder. |
| Momo | Static expressions; no ambient wandering or volunteered jokes. A deliberate tap can change the face/copy. | One visible focal Momo; gentle idle only while visible/idle. Rare ambient gesture, ≥45s apart; voluntary speech ≥60s apart and only when not busy. | Reuse safe-zone movement where already allowed, 20–36s gesture cadence; voluntary speech ≥28s apart and only when not busy. Never add roaming to Saved, Insights, Coach, forms or small-phone Today. |
| Sound / haptics | Only deliberate action cues if their separate toggles are on. No automatic arrival/reminder/milestone sound. | Existing short paired cues on actions, each respecting saved toggles. One cue per accepted/coalesced action. | Same cues and volume, not a louder audio mode. Optional extra visual character life does not add unsolicited sounds. |
| Reminders | Off unless explicitly enabled. | Off unless explicitly enabled. | Off unless explicitly enabled; no greater frequency. |

Provide an explicit **“Keep it quiet”** shortcut in the same preferences area: Calm + Sound off + Haptics off + reminder Off. Show the resulting settings; don't bury the behavior behind a mascot-only switch. People can then adjust channels independently. Mute Momo means no speech bubbles; Hide Momo means no mascot art across the product, including saved-log feedback and waiting states. Do not conflate either with sound or remove factual confirmation text.

OS reduced motion overrides every intensity for movement: no roaming, hops, canvas, spring travel, count-up, smooth auto-scroll or looping cues. Offer static expression changes and settled text. Saved Reduce Momo motion overrides all character placements, including celebration/analysis/wardrobe previews, while other allowed interface feedback can remain. Subscribe to live preference changes; cancel active/pending motion and scheduled cues when relevant toggles change.

Migration must preserve existing `mascotActivity` Calm/Lively/Off and independent channel preferences. The **new whole-app intensity defaults to Standard** when absent; an old Lively mascot value does not silently opt someone into whole-app Lively. Whole-app intensity is an upper bound on character movement/frequency: use the quieter of it and the saved mascot activity, then apply OS/saved reduced-motion and hide overrides. Thus choosing Lively doesn't override an existing Calm or Off mascot choice. Restore the previous mascot activity when Show Momo is re-enabled, rather than forcing Lively. Apply preferences after hydration and before feedback, including on direct routes and after account switches. Document new preference fields/defaults and their import/backup compatibility; no Expo UI redesign is part of this pass.

## 7. Measurement, budgets and acceptance

### Outcomes and guardrails

Measure useful returns and completed tasks, not session length, opens per hour, time in app or compulsive taps. These are proposed definitions and evaluation gates; **no uplift or current baseline is claimed**.

| Metric | Definition and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| **Next-day return (D1)** | Among eligible first-log users whose local day D+1 has elapsed, the share with a real foreground signed-in product use on local day D+1. Don't count welcome views, background retries or a backdated meal as a return. |
| **Seven-day return (D7)** | Among eligible first-log users whose D+7 has elapsed, the share using the product on local day D+7. Report an optional days-1–7 return window separately; don't label it D7. Exclude incomplete observation windows and synthetic/dev accounts. |
| **Logs per day** | Distribution of unique accepted entry IDs per actually active/logged local day, reported alongside total logged days. Exclude retry duplicates, imports and edits. A higher count is not automatically better and must not be driven by reward prompts. |
| **Time to first log** | Time from the first deliberate log-flow start to its accepted saved entry; use existing `first_log_started` and `entry_saved.duration_ms`, and separately report method, success/abandonment and retry/fallback rates. No time from eating/entered meal timestamp to saving: the resolved engagement plan rejects capture latency. Field usability and offline/manual paths matter more than a fast animation. |
| **Sound / haptics opt-out** | Share of exposed users who turn each channel off, plus preference-change counts and optional qualitative reasons. Report existing-off preferences separately from new opt-outs. |
| **Reminder opt-out** | Among users who explicitly enabled reminders, the share switching them off; report browser denial/unsupported state separately. There is no opt-out denominator for users never opted in. |
| **Quiet / Calm use** | Share choosing Calm or Keep it quiet, and usage under OS reduced motion. This is a preference signal, not failure; don't nudge people back to Standard. |

Current [analytics](../web/app/src/lib/analytics.ts) stores only the newest 200 allowlisted local events. It can already report entry-save flow duration; it does not have visit, intensity, preference or reminder events. [The telemetry contract](../packages/contracts/src/telemetry.ts) has no stable cohort identity, and `deliverRemoteTelemetry` is an unconditional no-op even if the frontend flag is set. **There is no existing population-retention dashboard.** Meal dates cannot substitute for visits, especially with backdating, imports, travel and offline saves.

Phase B can specify minimal local visit-day/presentation acknowledgement and preference diagnostics for consenting test participants. Proposed additions are local-day product-use, coarse intensity/channel changes, first-log outcome and deterministic moment type, not food text or nutrition. If event names/fields change, extend the strict shared contract and tests; don't send arbitrary event objects that it will silently reject. The 200-event buffer cannot prove complete seven-day cohorts; a local bounded day summary or consented study diary needs its own declared retention/deletion rule.

Any new storage key/field or remote measurement capability must update [retention](data/retention-schedule.md), [local-data inventory](data/local-data-inventory.md), disclosure and deletion tests before enablement. No email, DOB, food/photo content, body/weight values, Coach text, API keys or eating times enter new telemetry. Population/cohort collection requires a separate reviewed consent/identity/sink design; do not enable upload during a motion sprint. Local measurement and a consented study can proceed without a remote service.

Collect a pre-change baseline and compare two complete weekly cohorts with the same definitions, release and channel settings. Report denominators and uncertainty; do not claim a gain from a few anecdotes. Review higher opt-out/reminder complaint rates and user reports of pressure before interpreting a return increase. A higher Calm adoption is not a reason to roll back. Any lost/duplicate accepted entry, target/age/pause regression or new guilt/body-judgement copy is a stop condition regardless of retention.

### Explicit bundle and runtime budget

There is no current asset-size gate in [the Vite configuration](../web/app/vite.config.ts) or [build script](../web/app/scripts/build.mjs). Establish the production-build baseline at the approved Phase B starting commit; stale `dist` files are not a baseline.

| Budget | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| **Dependencies and assets** | Zero new runtime dependencies, fonts, remote animation assets, audio files or raster mascot payloads. Reuse Motion's async features, synthesized feel cues and shared Momo SVG. Don't move deferred features into eager imports. |
| **Transfer growth** | Relative to the same fresh base build: **≤10 KiB gzip added JavaScript in total across product chunks; ≤2 KiB gzip added CSS; ≤5 KiB gzip extra on the initial signed-in route**. Publish base/after per-chunk gzip sizes. No extra network request just to show feedback. |
| **Input and save** | No artificial delay before routing/saving. Press visual starts by the next frame; post-acceptance confirmation begins within **100ms p95** in the synthetic local test. Measure storage/network work separately. Coalescing must not schedule duplicate entry mutations. |
| **Runtime** | Target 60Hz: frame budget **16.7ms**, motion scripting **≤4ms/frame p95**, fewer than **5% missed frames**, and **zero animation-attributable long tasks >50ms** in the tested interaction traces. Confirm on a documented mid-range Android device; desktop CPU throttling is supplemental evidence only. |
| **Concurrent work** | One foreground event choreography; at most **six moving decorative elements**, with no canvas particles. At idle, one visible focal Momo with at most **two active motion tracks**. Offscreen/hidden document, editing and blocking dialogs stop ambient timers, pointer work and loops. |
| **Layout/accessibility** | Feedback-induced CLS **0**; controls do not move away from the pointer, ring/art slots reserve space, and only transform/opacity animate. Dynamic accepted content updates are immediate, not a height morph. Saved/OS reduced motion yields no decorative running animations or lingering timers. |

Profile ten repetitions of sheet open/close, Manual save, Saved relog/filter, first-day/ring feedback, wardrobe arrival and Coach send. Include long meal names, keyboard, dark mode, reduced motion and quiet settings. Cache pointer geometry outside frame loops; don't transplant `useMascotLife` onto every avatar. If a motion fails budget, simplify its choreography or use static confirmation before increasing any budget. Record device, release, method and trace; don't publish made-up measurements.
